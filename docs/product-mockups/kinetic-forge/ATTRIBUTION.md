# Asset attribution

## Mechanical chassis donor

- Asset: Mars 2020 Perseverance Rover GLB
- Source: NASA Science / NASA Jet Propulsion Laboratory
- Download: https://science.nasa.gov/3d-resources/mars-2020-perseverance-rover/
- Credit: NASA/JPL-Caltech
- Usage guidance: https://www.nasa.gov/nasa-brand-center/images-and-media/

The Kinetic Forge prototype removes the donor rover's scientific mast, wheels, robotic arm, antennae and mission markings from the visible assembly. It applies new industrial materials and combines the remaining mechanical geometry with original Kinetic Forge bodywork and moving wheel assemblies. NASA does not endorse this prototype.
